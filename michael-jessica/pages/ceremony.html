Stories from

It all began with a prayer. I trusted that, with God’s perfect timing, He would bring the right person into her life. I even boldly told my cousins I would bring a boyfriend to the upcoming wedding—despite being completely single at the time.

Through unexpected circumstances, God gently led me to Michael. What began as a simple friendship with someone in a similar stage of life soon grew into something deeper. We shared stories, laughter, and a connection neither expected.

Though I never imagined starting a relationship after knowing someone for only six months, with Michael, everything felt natural, peaceful, and right. Looking back, I realized God had been answering my prayer all along. What began with faith became friendship, which then blossomed into love, and today, we joyfully begin a new chapter together, trusting in God’s continued guidance and blessing.

In 2018, I moved to Canada with a clear purpose—to pursue higher education, build a better future, and eventually become a Permanent Resident. A relationship was never part of my plan; my heart was focused on adapting to a new country and pursuing the dreams God had placed before me.

Then came the COVID-19 pandemic. While the world felt more isolated than ever, God was quietly preparing a story I never expected.

Through my housemate, Monica, I was introduced to Jessica. What began as simple conversations grew into a meaningful friendship built on shared values, faith, and understanding. Before I knew it, our friendship blossomed into something more.

After much prayer and seeking God’s guidance, I took a step of faith and asked Jessica to begin a relationship. Trusting in God’s perfect timing, our love story officially began.

Growing together

Five years together have been a beautiful journey of love, growth, and learning. Through joyful moments, challenges, misunderstandings, and disagreements, we discovered the importance of patience, forgiveness, communication, and knowing when to speak or simply listen. With God’s guidance, we learned to release unrealistic expectations and focus on giving their best to one another.

Their journey has shaped their understanding of true love—not as perfection, but as a daily choice to trust, respect, appreciate, and support each other. we have learned to embrace their differences as blessings, complementing and strengthening one another. Through every season, we continue choosing each other and growing together.

- Michael & Jessica -
